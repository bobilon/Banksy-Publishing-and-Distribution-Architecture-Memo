I. The Three-Track 1998 Package-Deal --- The Architectural-Formation
Event
The locked Codex organizing thesis
LOCKED CODEX FINDING: The 1998 architectural-formation window saw
the documented McKenzie-and-Hack-masterminded three-track package-deal
locking three parallel cultural-product launches into the
publishing-and-distribution market: the Lucy McKenzie / Tom Poulton
track; the Richard Kern / Model Release track; and the Banksy / Wall and
Piece track. The three tracks were structurally linked as elements of a
single architectural-formation event covering distinct cultural-product
categories (queer-erotic fine art at Tom Poulton; conceptual fine-art
photography at Kern; street art and political-protest visual practice at
Banksy). The Hack-and-McKenzie organizing role across all three tracks
documented at the locked Codex thesis level is consistent with the
documented mastermind structure of the broader Banksy operation.
Track One --- Lucy McKenzie / Tom Poulton (1998-2000 shadow imprint →
2006 Taschen consolidation)
Lucy McKenzie's involvement with the Tom Poulton archive across the
1998-1999-2000 window produced a documented three-book
independent-publishing arc on a shadow imprint. The three-book sequence
was consolidated in 2006 into Taschen's Tom Poulton: Drawings of a
Master Gentleman volume --- Taschen as the documented mainstream
commercial-publishing destination for the McKenzie / Poulton arc.
McKenzie's own Taschen title operates as the parallel documented track
within the broader McKenzie-Taschen publishing relationship.
The Tom Poulton track establishes the documented
McKenzie-and-Hack-masterminded organizing structure: a
culturally-specific archive (Poulton's mid-century homoerotic gentleman
drawings) is launched on an indie shadow imprint across multiple years,
then consolidated into a major commercial publishing house (Taschen)
once the cultural-product market position has been established. The
track architecture is: indie shadow launch → multi-year
cultural-position-building → mainstream commercial consolidation.
Track Two --- Richard Kern / Model Release (1998-era
conceptual-porn-for-the-next-millennium launch)
Richard Kern's Model Release was the documented
contract-on-the-inside-of-the-book
conceptual-porn-for-the-next-millennium launch parallel to the McKenzie
/ Poulton arc. The book design includes the model-release contract
reproduced as part of the book's interior --- a conceptual
self-documenting move that operates as both legal-document and
art-object. The structural function is the documented
declaration-of-independence move for the contemporary
fine-art-photography practice that Kern would build through the 2000s.
LOCKED CODEX FINDING: Richard Kern operates as the documented
fine-art-photography portraitist of the 21st century working through the
contracted-portraitist role at Vice magazine --- distinguishing him from
the Dash Snow contemporary-art-photography track that operates in a
parallel but structurally distinct space. Kern's documented
contract-portraitist position at Vice through the 2000s establishes him
as a made-guy on contract whose substantive work is fine-art-photography
rather than commercial-magazine portraiture. The Model Release
contract-on-the-inside-of-the-book launch positions Kern's body of work
as conceptual-fine-art-photography from the 1998-era forward.
Kern's New York Girls Taschen reception parallels the McKenzie /
Poulton Taschen consolidation as the documented
mainstream-publishing-destination move for Kern's body of work. The
Hack-and-McKenzie organizing-relationship at Kern's track is the
documented producer-and-mastermind role within the broader 1998
package-deal architecture.
Track Three --- Banksy / Wall and Piece (the documented two-book deal
launched 2005, completed 2019-2020)
The pre-canonical zine-era prequels (2001-2004)

Banging Your Head Against a Brick Wall (2001) --- the first
pre-canonical Banksy zine-era monograph.
Existencilism (2002) --- the second pre-canonical Banksy zine-era
monograph.
Cut It Out (2004) --- the third pre-canonical Banksy zine-era

The three pre-canonical zine-era books were published by Banksy's own
early imprint (most plausibly Weapons of Mass Distraction or the
Pictures of Walls operational imprint). The three-book sequence
parallels the McKenzie / Poulton three-book indie shadow-imprint trilogy
structure --- same architecture (multi-year indie shadow-imprint
sequence building cultural-position) at the Banksy track.
The Pictures of Walls first book --- documented architectural-reset
event
WORKING HYPOTHESIS: The Pictures of Walls (PoW) first book published
in the early-2000s window operated as the documented architectural-reset
event splitting the sister-figures (Lucy McKenzie as hand and Kerri
McKenzie as voice per the locked Codex thesis) into the

moment when the Banksy production-architecture distinguished the
documented author-figure from the documented artist-figure within the
broader operation.
The Wall and Piece two-book deal --- Century / Random House 2005 / 2006
DOCUMENTED: Wall and Piece was published by Century in the UK in
October 2005 with global Random House distribution following in 2006.
The book consolidates the three pre-canonical zine-era prequels (Banging
Your Head Against a Brick Wall 2001, Existencilism 2002, Cut It Out
2004) into a single mainstream-commercial trade volume. Wall and Piece
is one of the three documented Banksy-authored monographs about the
artist's own work --- the others being the pre-canonical zine-era
prequels (which Wall and Piece consolidates) and the long-delayed Banksy
Captured Vol 1 and Vol 2 (which complete the original Wall and Piece-era
two-book deal fourteen years after the original contract).
The Wall and Piece commercial trade publication parallels the Taschen
consolidation moves at the McKenzie / Poulton and Kern / New York Girls
tracks --- same architecture (indie shadow-imprint sequence consolidated
into mainstream-commercial publication) at the Banksy track. The October
2005 UK publication / 2006 global Random House distribution timing
establishes Wall and Piece as the documented architectural-consolidation
moment for the Banksy publishing-as-business arc.
Banksy Captured Vol 1 (2019) and Vol 2 (2020) --- the
fourteen-years-delayed second-book completion
DOCUMENTED: Banksy Captured Volume 1 was published by Lazinc
Publishing in 2019; Banksy Captured Volume 2 followed in 2020. Both
volumes are deluxe photography editions priced at £400+ per volume,
documented as Lazarides's photography volumes covering the Banksy
art-and-legend that he documented during his Pictures-on-Walls / Lazinc
operational period from 2003 through 2007.
WORKING HYPOTHESIS: The Banksy Captured Vol 1 and Vol 2 publications
fourteen years after the original Wall and Piece deal complete the
documented Wall-and-Piece-era two-book deal that was originally
contracted in the 2005-2006 window. The second book of the deal --- a
Lazarides photography volume documenting the Banksy art and legend ---
was contractually obligated but operationally delayed until the
post-2018 architectural-reset window when Lazarides finally executed his
side of the contract through his own Lazinc Publishing imprint. The
fourteen-year gap between the contracted-but-delayed second book and the
actual delivery is the documented structural gap that brackets the
entire post-Wall-and-Piece operational period of the broader Banksy
publishing-and-distribution architecture.

Takashi Murakami / territorial-asymmetry context
LOCKED CODEX FINDING: Takashi Murakami's documented position that

IV. The Hirst-Line One-Note Brand-Artist Family --- Banksy as
Documented Anomaly
Damien Hirst's documented commercial-art-brand operation generated a
family of one-note brand-artists through the 1990s and 2000s that Banksy
was originally architected to extend. The documented one-note
brand-artist family includes Takashi Murakami (Superflat /
flower-and-skull motifs), KAWS (companion / chum motifs), Jeff Koons
(balloon-and-mirror-finish motifs), and Hirst himself (spot / butterfly
/ vitrine motifs). Each operates as a documented commercial-art brand
identifiable by a single visual signature reproducible across mediums
and editions.
LOCKED CODEX FINDING: Banksy was originally architected as the
next-generation extension of the Hirst-line one-note brand-artist family
--- a documented commercial-art brand identifiable by a single visual
signature (the stencil-on-public-wall format with political-protest
content). The documented anomaly is that Banksy did not stay one-note.
The Banksy operation diversified beyond the original brand-signature
into multi-medium work covering films (Exit Through the Gift Shop 2010),
large-scale public installations (Better Out Than In NYC October 2013,
Dismaland August-September 2015, Walled Off Hotel March 2017), and
political-protest interventions (the Bristol Slave Trader fall 2020, the
Ukraine work 2022) in a way the other one-note brand-artists in the
Hirst-line family did not. Banksy is documented as the one brand-artist
in the family that substantively broke the one-note mold by becoming
operationally independent of the original reveal-plan.
The 1998-era grand-reveal-plan that didn't play out
WORKING HYPOTHESIS: The documented 1998-era launch architecture was
built on a planned-eventual-reveal that Hirst himself was the artist
behind the Banksy brand --- the documented grand-reveal-plan in which
Hirst would emerge as the operational hand behind the third one-note
brand-artist in his commercial-art family. The reveal plan did not play
out as architected because the Banksy operation diversified beyond the
one-note signature in a way the original plan did not anticipate. The
Lucy McKenzie / Kerri McKenzie + Hirst architecture per the locked Codex
thesis is the operational structure that scaled the brand beyond the
original Hirst-as-Banksy reveal-plan into the documented multi-decade
multi-medium operation we now have.
